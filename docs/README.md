# diMail documentation

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): install, the Google client, sign in, the first sync, turning on AI.
- [Creating your Google client](google-client.md): step by step in the Google Cloud console, for one person or for a company on Google Workspace. Also [in Italian](it/google-client.md).
- [User guide](user-guide.md): the inbox as a to-do list, Important and Other, sections, conversations, composing, search, and every keyboard shortcut.
- [AI](ai.md): Mistral and OpenAI, your key, models, the daily budget, what is sent.
- [Privacy](privacy.md): where your mail is stored, what leaves your Mac and where it goes.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): the most common problems and their fixes.

## Under the hood
